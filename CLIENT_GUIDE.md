# HMB Ispat — Executive Dashboard User Guide

Welcome to the **HMB Ispat Executive Intelligence Portal**. This platform gives you real-time visibility into sales performance, dispatch metrics, dealer analytics, and pending order backlogs across all your assigned territories.

---

## 🔑 How to Log In

1. **Open the Dashboard Link**: Navigate to the portal URL provided by your administrator.
2. **Enter Your Username**: Type your full registered name as listed in the user directory (e.g., `Prakash Roy`, `Kaushik`, `Arindam`, `Madhav`).
3. **Enter Your Password**: Your default password is your **Name without spaces** followed by **`@26`**.

## 📊 What You Will See (Dashboard Features)

Once logged in, the system automatically tailors the view to your assigned territory:

### 1. 📈 Executive Overview
- **Key Metrics**: View total dispatch volume in Metric Tons (MT), month-over-month growth percentage, and active dealer counts.
- **Product Breakdown**: See volume distribution across core product lines (IG, GI, IGG, P, RS, SS).
- **Target Tracking**: Track actual dispatches against monthly target goals.

### 2. 🗺️ State & District Performance
- **Regional Ranking**: See which states and districts are leading in volume dispatches.
- **Detailed Metrics**: View total metric tons, current vs. previous cycle performance, and percentage share.

### 3. 🏪 Dealer Performance
- **Client Accounts**: Monitor active dealer orders and volume contributions.
- **Backlog Tracking**: Identify dealers with open pending orders and check clearance rates.

### 4. 🧠 AI Insights & Actions
- **Operational Highlights**: Automated summaries pointing out key growth drivers and volume shifts.
- **Actionable Guidance**: Recommended next steps and priorities for field teams and sales managers.

### 5. 🚨 Alerts & Risk
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Instant visibility into high-priority alerts, delayed dispatches, or declining dealer volumes.

### 6. 🌐 Geographic View (Interactive Map)
- **Visual Map**: Interactive geographical map showing dispatch density and activity across states and districts.
- **Hover & Click**: Hover over any region to view instant volume details, or click a state to zoom into its district breakdown.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use the Controls & Filters

- **Despatch vs. Pending Toggle**: Click **Despatch** to view completed deliveries, or **Pending** to view current open order backlogs.
- **Viewing Month Filter**: Use the dropdown to select current month (MTD), previous month, or historical months.
- **Product Chips**: Click on product tags (IG, GI, IGG, P, RS, SS) to filter the whole dashboard by specific product lines.
- **Light / Dark Mode**: Toggle between Day (Corporate Navy Light) and Night (Warm Charcoal) themes using the **Sun / Moon** button at the bottom of the left sidebar.
